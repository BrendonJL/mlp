## Callbacks Enhancement

Added proper multi-stage distance tracking to both `WandbCallback` and `DatabaseCallback`:

**New tracking variables:**
- `accumulated_distance` - Sum of completed stage lengths
- `max_x_pos` - Highest total distance reached during episode
- `starting_stage` - Stage when episode began (for level_completed detection)
- `prev_stage` / `prev_x_pos` - Previous step values for transition detection

**New logged metrics:**
- `max_x_pos` - Maximum distance reached (accounts for stage transitions)
- `final_x_pos` - Final accumulated distance
- `level_completed` - Boolean based on stage change (not just flag_get)
